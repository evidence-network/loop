Watch the network

This page is the evidence network's. It draws the network as a graph, one day at a time, from the same committed files as the stats page: the scrubbed day files, the commitment list in nodes/ and the loop's findings. Nothing on it is made up: an edge kind with no finding for the shown day says so.

    • answered: a reply came back (solid, dot)
    • refused: a signed refusal (dashed, bar)
    • absent: nothing came back (dotted, ring)
    • 05, 06, 07 on a thicker edge between two nodes: a deal, a settlement or a later ask, from that step's finding

    The reporter sits in the middle and asks each listed node two questions; each ask is an edge to the node. Tab to a node, or hover it, for its details. A node is ringed on the first day it is in a day file; a node that left is faded and dashed. Nodes keep their place on the circle by node id; the order means nothing.

    The same counts as text

    Live and rehearsal are two sets, never added together. Each count is out of what it counts: the nodes the reporter listed that day, or the asks it made. "Not measured yet" names the loop step whose finding would hold that edge kind.

    How to read this page

    What the edges are, and what they are not

    Each day the reporter asks every node on the commitment list the same two questions, checkpoints and history_card/1. Each question is one edge. It is drawn answered when a reply came back (verified, no_checkpoint_yet, no_announced_key or not_evidence), refused for a signed refusal, and absent for an absence. The stats page says what each result means.

    The loop's steps 05 (deal), 06 (settle) and 07 (ask) are node-to-node edges. They are drawn only from a finding of that step for the shown day, and only between two nodes of the shown set. Until a step has a finding, the page says "not measured yet" for its edges. There is no amount on this page, and no score, ranking or table of nodes by result.

    The rehearsal is a test of the reporter: the same questions to the same nodes every 10 minutes, one slot per step of the slider. It is never drawn on the same graph as the daily set.

    Built by site/watch.py. The data the graph draws is data.json, also carried in this page.
