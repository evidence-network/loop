Watch the network

Hover a node, or Tab to it, for its card.

  • dotted: two listed nodes, no exchange in a committed finding that day
  • solid: a finding of step 05, 06 or 07 shows the two exchanged; the numbers say which
  • ring, solid: the reporter's ask was answered
  • ring, dashed: a signed refusal
  • ring, dotted: nothing came back
  • badge: history cards stapled to the node's commitment
  • outer rings: a community its cards cover, mesh-llm, personal agents or goose, each its own colour
  • thin ring: that community's card has no history yet, a new stream or one started again
  • "bound": nodes one controller binds, whose binding a tool pinned in the lock verifies, drawn as one dot

Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, Shift and drag to rotate. With the graph focused: + and − zoom, the arrow keys pan, [ and ] rotate, 0 resets. Tab moves between the nodes; Esc clears the card.

This page is the evidence network's. It draws the network as a graph, one day at a time, from the same committed files as the stats page: the scrubbed day files, the commitment list in nodes/, the nodes' committed commitments and the loop's findings. Nothing on it is made up: an edge kind with no finding for the shown day says so.

Each node listed in the shown day's file is a dot, in its day state, with two rings: the reporter's two asks to it, inner checkpoints, outer history_card/1. Every two nodes are joined by a dotted line; the line is solid where a finding shows the two exchanged. A node has a bright edge on its first day in a day file; a node that left is faded and dashed. Where a node first sits comes from its node id; the place means nothing.

Counts over time

One count per chart, from the same data as the graph, a point per day (or rehearsal slot). An on-demand run is its own hollow point, labelled with its time, never part of a day's count. Live and rehearsal are never added together: each chart belongs to the set shown. A count with no finding yet reads "not measured yet".

How to read this page

What the lines and rings are, and what they are not

Each day the reporter asks every node on the commitment list the same two questions, checkpoints and history_card/1. Each question is a ring on the node. It is drawn answered when a reply came back (verified, no_checkpoint_yet, no_announced_key or not_evidence), refused for a signed refusal, and absent for an absence. The stats page says what each result means.

Any two listed nodes can share evidence, so every pair is drawn, dotted. The loop's steps 05 (deal), 06 (settle) and 07 (ask) make a pair solid, only from a finding of that step for the shown day, and only between two nodes of the shown set. Until a step has a finding, the page says "not measured yet" for it. There is no amount on this page, and no score, ranking or table of nodes by result.

A node's badge counts the history cards stapled to its commitment, as committed, one per evidence stream. Its card lists each one's stream and the witnesses whose receipts the card carries, or "unwitnessed". Each community the cards cover is a ring round the node. A card with no history yet draws its ring thin, with the day its stream started: a new stream and one started again look the same.

Nodes that one controller binds are drawn as one dot, labelled "bound", only when a binding verdict says verified and names a verifier pinned in the lock this page was built with. Otherwise each node is its own dot, and its card says why they are not drawn as one.

The rehearsal is a test of the reporter: the same questions to the same nodes every 10 minutes, one slot per step of the slider. It is never drawn on the same graph as the daily set.
